Teewai AI Dental Whiteness Companion
Project Description
One-pager

A 0-to-100 whiteness index from a single tooth photo.

Teewai is an AI-powered dental health app that turns a smartphone photo of your teeth into an instant, clinically-inspired whiteness reading — and tracks how your daily diet stains your enamel over time.

Purpose

Professional shade assessment is expensive, subjective, and rarely accessible between dental visits. Teewai democratizes it: a single photo is analyzed with computer vision and mapped to the VITA shade guide, giving everyone a free, repeatable 0–100 whiteness score they can track like a wellness metric.

Beyond a number, Teewai connects the score to its cause — logging the chromogenic, tannin, and acid load of what you eat and drink — so users see exactly which habits brighten or stain their smile, and can act on it.

Key Features

10–100 Whiteness Index

A single, trackable score derived from enamel color analysis — the dental equivalent of a step count.

2VITA Shade Mapping

Each scan is matched to the clinical VITA classical shade scale, bridging AI output and dentist language.

3Dietary Stain Tracking

Log meals to see chromogenic, tannin, and acid loads and how they nudge your shade up or down.

4AI Care Plan

Personalized, plain-language recommendations to protect and improve whiteness between visits.

5Scan History & Trends

Every scan is saved with its photo, score, and shade, so progress is visible over days and weeks.

6Private by Default

Photos are stored privately and processed securely — your smile never becomes public content.
