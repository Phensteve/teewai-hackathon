teewai — Dental Analysis & Whitening Tracker

teewai scores your smile's whiteness and health from a single photo. Snap a photo and teewai's AI extracts whiteness, shade, stain and dental health signals; a deterministic engine then maps them to a VITA Classical shade, a 0-100 whiteness score, and a dental health score. You get an instant shade match, stain and plaque detection, a personalized whitening plan backed by research, and a progress tracker that shows your improvement over time. Snap your meals and teewai identifies the chromogens, tannins and acids in your diet, rolling each into a daily stain load that shows exactly what's dimming your smile. Family plans include 5 members with 15 scans each per month, tracked individually. We use your Google account only to sign you in securely and create your profile.
